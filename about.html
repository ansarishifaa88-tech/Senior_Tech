About Senior Tech

Senior Tech is a digital literacy website created to help senior citizens learn and use everyday digital technology with confidence.

Our Purpose

Many senior citizens may find smartphones, WhatsApp, digital payments and online safety difficult to understand. Senior Tech provides simple, clear and step-by-step guidance for these everyday digital tasks.

What You Can Learn

The website covers smartphone basics, WhatsApp, digital payments and UPI, and online safety. Users can also test their knowledge through the quiz.

Designed for Senior Citizens

Senior Tech uses simple language, clear instructions, readable text and accessibility options such as font-size controls, dark mode and read-aloud support.
