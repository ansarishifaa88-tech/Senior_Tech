Digital Payments & UPI

Learn the basic steps for using UPI and digital payments safely.

1. What is UPI?

UPI allows you to send and receive money using a smartphone and a bank account.

2. Pay Using a QR Code

Open your UPI payment app, scan the QR code, enter the correct amount and check the receiver's details before making the payment.

3. Keep Your UPI PIN Secret

Your UPI PIN is private. Never share it with anyone, even if someone asks for it over a phone call or message.

4. Never Share OTP

Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details with another person. Genuine support staff should not ask you to reveal these secrets.
